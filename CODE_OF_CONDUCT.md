# Code of Conduct

SliceBridge is a community project for people who use different printers, slicers and platforms.

## Expectations

- Be respectful, patient and constructive; disagreement about tools or approaches is welcome.
- Give useful technical feedback without personal attacks, harassment or discriminatory language.
- Respect privacy, permissions and intellectual-property rights. Do not publish anyone's personal information or proprietary models.
- Keep issue and PR discussions focused on the work. Avoid spam or promotional content.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove content, close discussions or restrict participation when behavior undermines these expectations. GitHub's platform policies also apply.

If you encounter abuse on GitHub, use GitHub's built-in **Report content** mechanism or [GitHub's abuse reporting guidance](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/acceptable-use-policies/github-acceptable-use-policies). Do not post someone's private details in a public conduct report. For security vulnerabilities, use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.
