# Support / תמיכה

**SliceBridge is in development and is not yet available as a supported general release.**

## English

- **Bug or compatibility issue:** [Open a bug report](https://github.com/nocturney/slicebridge/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).
- **Idea or improvement:** [Request a feature](https://github.com/nocturney/slicebridge/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
- **Development status:** [Open issues and pull requests](https://github.com/nocturney/slicebridge/issues).
- **Security concern:** Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting, not a public issue.

For bug reports, provide the app version/commit, operating system, source slicer, target slicer/printer, physical nozzle and material, steps to reproduce, and the exact error when possible. **Do not upload a private 3MF, customer model, credentials or machine-specific secrets publicly.** If you can, create a minimal synthetic reproduction instead.

Responses and fixes are best-effort; there is no SLA while the app is in development.
